
# Product Requirements Document: Evernight Oath: Dawn Chronicles (永夜之誓：破曉紀錄)

## 1. Project Overview
*   **Game Title:** Evernight Oath: Dawn Chronicles
*   **Genre:** Dark Fantasy Action RPG (ARPG)
*   **Platform:** Desktop / PC (Cross-platform potential)
*   **Target Audience:** Fans of dark fantasy, challenging ARPG combat, and deep narrative-driven management systems.
*   **Art Style:** Dark horror, high-contrast lighting (Chiaroscuro), gothic architecture, and visceral visual effects.

## 2. Core Pillars
### 2.1 Dual-Form Combat
Players toggle between **Radiant Form** (high burst, range, and crowd control) and **Shadow Form** (high mobility, assassination, and lifesteal). Combat is built around managing these two energy states and executing "Form Shifts" at peak energy for devastating effects.

### 2.2 Dynamic Light & Darkness Mechanics
Light is a survival resource. 
*   **Light Zones:** Safe, normal visibility.
*   **Dark Zones:** High risk (lethal enemies, low visibility) but high reward (rare materials, increased critical hit rates in Shadow Form).
*   **Lantern Management:** Players must gather fuel to maintain their light source during expeditions.

### 2.3 Citadel Management & Morality
Between expeditions, players manage the last bastion of humanity. 
*   **Resource Management:** Rations, Black Iron, and Morale.
*   **Moral Dilemmas:** Urgent decisions (e.g., how to handle a plague outbreak) that branch the story and impact city survival.

### 2.4 Ancestral Forge (Gacha)
A high-fidelity summoning system for legendary weapons and companions. Features a 65-pull pity system for SSR weapons and a "Resonance" system for progression.

## 3. Key Systems & Features
### 3.1 Weapon Classes
1.  **Greatsword (🗡️):** Heavy impact, "Dawnbreaker" archetype.
2.  **Dual Blades (⚔️):** High DPS, backstab mechanics.
3.  **Spear (🔱):** Mid-range spacing and thrusts.
4.  **Warhammer (🔨):** Shield-breaking and stuns.
5.  **Crossbow (🏹):** Ranged kiting and status effects.
6.  **Scythe (🌙):** Crowd control and area lifesteal.

### 3.2 Progression: The Arsenal Sanctum
*   **Refinement:** Sacrificing duplicates to increase base stats.
*   **Talent Resonance:** A node-based tree where players unlock weapon-specific skills (e.g., "Sunbeam Reach") using specialized currencies.

### 3.3 Companion System
Players deploy with one of four classes: Frontline Guardian, Shadow Stalker, Scholar of Secrets, or Night-Eater. Bonds are strengthened through Citadel interactions.

## 4. Boss Encounter Design (The Pillar Demons)
Bosses utilize environmental lighting shifts. 
*   **Example: Garuka, the Bone-Gaw.** Extinguishes arena lights to enter a berserk state. Players must use companions to relight braziers to create "Sanctified Zones" to stun the boss.

## 5. Visual Identity & UI
*   **Color Palette:** Deep blacks, obsidian grays, accented by radiant gold (#FFD700) and blood crimson (#8B0000).
*   **Typography:** EB Garamond for a gothic, historical feel.
*   **HUD Design:** Minimalist but thematic, using curved Gauges for HP/Energy to maintain immersion in the dark world.

## 6. Development Roadmap (Phases)
1.  **Phase 1 (Prototype):** Core combat feel, dual-form switching, and basic Lantern mechanics.
2.  **Phase 2 (Content):** First Forbidden Zone (Barren Wastes) and Boss Garuka.
3.  **Phase 3 (Citadel):** Base management UI and morality event system.
4.  **Phase 4 (Gacha & Social):** Ancestral Forge implementation and companion bonding.
5.  **Phase 5 (Polishing):** High-end VFX, lighting optimization, and balancing.
